Section 01

What is Faven?

Faven — Food Haven — is India's first verified food experience platform. Think Instagram, but built entirely around food — and where every post is tied to proof that the creator was actually there.

It is not a restaurant directory. Not an influencer platform. Not another review app. Faven is trust infrastructure for the food internet — a platform that rewards authentic, verified food experiences with credibility, reach, and real money.

Faven is proudly Indian. Built on UPI — the payment backbone of a billion people. Built for the collectivist dining culture where food is memory, identity, and community. Built for the 180 million urban Indians who already post food content every day and earn absolutely nothing for it.

Faven is not for professionals only. It is for the student who photographs their biriyani at a local joint. The family documenting their weekend lunch. The solo traveller who found a hidden gem. The micro creator with 500 followers who knows more about food than any influencer with 500,000. Every one of them belongs on Faven. Every one of them deserves to be paid for being real.

Section 04

How Faven Works — The Verification System

Every review on Faven is tied to proof of physical presence using a five-signal layered stack. No single signal is required to be perfect — verification is a tiered confidence score, not a binary pass or fail.

1

UPI Payment Verification Primary

When a creator pays for their meal, a UPI payment confirmation with a Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) number is automatically generated on their phone. Faven validates the UTR format, cross-references the merchant name against the restaurant via Google Places API, and confirms the timestamp. The creator did nothing extra. The proof generated itself.

2

Photo EXIF Metadata Primary

Every photo taken on a smartphone automatically embeds GPS coordinates and a timestamp into the image file. Faven silently reads this when a photo is uploaded and cross-references the location against the restaurant's known coordinates via Google Places API. The creator took a photo of their food — which they were going to do anyway. Verification happened inside the photo before they thought about posting.

3

Receipt OCR Fallback

For cash payments, creators upload a receipt photo. Faven reads the restaurant name and date using OCR via Google Cloud Vision or Amazon Textract. Covers the significant segment of India that still pays cash at local restaurants.

4

AI Photo Authenticity Detection Baseline

Every uploaded photo is scanned. Three outcomes: fully AI-generated images are rejected outright; legitimately AI-enhanced real photos are allowed with a transparent disclosure label; significantly manipulated photos go to the internal trust and safety team. Content Authenticity Initiative (Adobe CAI) integration adopted as the standard becomes mainstream across camera apps.

5

Community Corroboration Cultural

When multiple users post reviews of the same restaurant within the same time window from the same GPS cluster, their individual verification scores mutually reinforce each other. Built for India's collectivist dining culture — groups of friends, families, colleagues dining together. Also a viral growth mechanic: one person at the table tells their friends to post so everyone's score improves, onboarding four new users at a single lunch at zero cost.

Tiered Confidence Scoring

Signals PresentBadge AssignedReach Impact
4–5 signals✅ Fully VerifiedMaximum algorithmic boost
2–3 signals🟡 Partially VerifiedModerate boost
AI scan only⬜ ReviewedStandard reach

Nobody gets zero. Every honest creator gets something. Scores improve naturally over time as creators build a consistent posting history — the platform learns to trust them even without perfect signals on every post.

Sponsored Visit Handling

Sponsored visits are handled transparently — a Sponsored Visit toggle is available on every post. EXIF metadata still fires since the creator was physically present. An optional restaurant confirmation flow lets the establishment verify the creator was their guest. An optional booking screenshot lets creators attach their invitation message as supplementary evidence. A sponsored verified review on Faven is more transparent than any sponsored post on Instagram — because on Faven, consumers always know which is which.

Moderation — Two Layers Only

Automated checks handle 90–95% of all cases through technical verification requiring no human judgement. A small paid internal trust and safety team — funded within the seed round — handles edge cases and appeals. Trained, consistent, accountable, and completely independent of the creator community. No community moderation. No conflict of interest. No bias.

Search

Keyword search across all verified reviews at launch — standard full text search using MySQL or Elasticsearch. Two days of development work, immediately useful from the first review posted. Semantic search (understanding what "aesthetic" means, not just matching the word) launches in Phase 3. Visual AI analysis of photos for aesthetic cues — cosy, vibrant, romantic — launches in Phase 4, using the growing verified photo library as a training dataset.

The Reward System — Earning From Day One

A credibility score alone is not enough to make someone download a new app and post their first review. Faven delivers four layers of immediate, tangible value from the moment a user joins.

💸

Every user's first verified post triggers ₹25 UPI cashback — immediately, into their phone. Real money. Shareable screenshot. Word-of-mouth moment that costs ₹25 per organically acquired user.

🔥

Weekly Streak Coins

Faven Coins earned for consecutive weekly posting accumulate toward Pro access, cashback thresholds, and brand vouchers. Mechanic users already understand from Swiggy and Amazon Pay.

🏆

Monthly City Leaderboard

Top 10 verified reviewers in each city win real prizes each month. Small prize pool. Massive organic engagement. Creators share their rank. Followers download to compete.

🎟️

Restaurant Voucher Milestones

At 5, 10, and 20 verified posts, users unlock vouchers from partner restaurants — free dessert, 20% off, complimentary drink. Self-perpetuating loop: the reward funds the next verified post.

Section 06

Business Model — How Faven Makes Money

Faven sells to creators — not restaurants. This is the Meta/YouTube model: the free tier builds the network, the paid tier monetises power users. Proven at billion-dollar scale. Applied to the food creator economy for the first time.

Restaurants are not the customer. They are the subject matter. This is critical — it means Faven's revenue never depends on convincing restaurants to care about authenticity, which they largely don't. Revenue comes from creators who want to grow, which is universal and proven.

Primary Revenue — Creator Subscriptions

See Creator Tiers section below. Recurring, predictable, platform-controlled revenue that rises every month as more creators subscribe and renew.

Secondary Revenue — Brand Campaign Marketplace (Phase 3)

F&B brands pay Faven to run verified food trail campaigns. Pro and Elite creators opt in. Faven takes a platform cut. Creators earn. Brands get real content from verified visitors — something no other platform can offer.

Tertiary Revenue — B2B Data Licensing (Phase 4)

Aggregated, anonymised verified visit data licensed to food delivery platforms, tourism boards, and real estate developers. High margin. Zero incremental cost per deal.

Supplementary Revenue — City Guide Listings

Restaurants optionally pay a small fee to appear in Faven's curated verified city guides. Optional, not required — they come to Faven because the audience is worth it, not because we sold them a philosophy.

Section 08

Phased Rollout Strategy

Verification is Faven's core differentiator — but enforcing it on day one creates friction that kills early adoption. Faven rolls out gradually, turning verification from a gate into a reward.

Phase 1 · Month 0–3

Open Platform

No verification enforced. Anyone posts freely. Verification is an optional prompt with a clear one-screen explanation of its benefits. Seed 50 founding Elite creators across Bangalore with free access for 3 months — no conditions attached. ₹25 cashback, streak coins, and city leaderboard active from day one to build habit before enforcement begins.

Success metric: 2,000 creators onboarded. 500 verified reviews posted. Leaderboard has genuine competition.

Phase 2 · Month 3–6

Voluntary Verification + Pro Launch

Verified content receives algorithmically higher reach — a visible, measurable gap that creates FOMO. Pro and Elite subscriptions launch. Restaurant voucher milestone rewards activate. Restaurant confirmation flow launches. Community corroboration scoring activates.

Success metric: 10,000 creators. 500 paying Pro subscribers. ₹2.5 lakh MRR. First brand campaign pilot running.

Phase 3 · Month 6–12

Verified-First Algorithm

Verified content gets significant algorithmic priority in discovery, search, and city guides. Brand campaign marketplace launches. Semantic search launches. Expansion to Mumbai and Delhi.

Success metric: 25,000 creators across 3 cities. 2,000 Pro + 200 Elite subscribers. ₹12 lakh MRR.

Phase 4 · Month 12–18

Full Enforcement + Scale

Verification required for all new submissions. B2B data licensing launches. Visual AI aesthetic photo analysis development begins — using the verified photo library as training data. Content Authenticity Initiative integration deepens. Southeast Asia expansion planning begins.

Success metric: 50,000 creators across India. 5,000 Pro + 500 Elite subscribers. ₹35 lakh+ MRR. Series A ready.

Marketing Strategy

Core principle: Faven does not need paid advertising to grow. Creators are the distribution channel. Every piece of verified content posted on Faven is simultaneously a piece of marketing for Faven on every other platform where that creator shares it.

Pre-Launch — Seed Creator Campaign

The single most important marketing activity Faven will ever do. Before public launch, personally identify and onboard 50 micro food creators across Bangalore. Selection criteria: genuine food enthusiasm, consistent posting history, reputation for honest content. Give every one of them free Elite access for 3 months — no conditions, no obligations. Their genuine enthusiasm is more credible than any paid post. These 50 creators collectively reach hundreds of thousands of food enthusiasts. That is the launch audience at zero cost.

The ₹25 Screenshot — Word of Mouth Engine

Every time a new user receives their ₹25 cashback they screenshot it and share it on WhatsApp and Instagram stories. That screenshot is Faven's most powerful marketing asset — real proof that Faven pays people as an immediate lived experience, not a future promise. Cost per organically acquired user: ₹25.

FOMO Mechanics

City leaderboards create visible public competition. The verified badge creates social FOMO among unverified creators who see verified ones getting measurably better reach. The streak system builds return-visit habit. The product mechanics create desire without any ad spend.

Group Dining Viral Loop

One person at a restaurant table tells their friends to post too — because group posting from the same GPS cluster raises everyone's verification score. Four new users onboarded at a single lunch. Zero marketing spend. Uniquely possible in India's collectivist dining culture.

Creator Affiliate Programme (Phase 2)

Creators who refer other creators earn bonus Faven Coins for every referred creator who posts their first verified review. Simple, trackable, incentivised. Turns every Faven creator into a recruitment channel.

Two Messages, One Platform

To creators: "You've been posting food photos for free for years. Faven pays you for it — from your very first verified post. No follower threshold. No algorithm lottery. Just real food, real visits, real money."

To consumers: "Tired of fake restaurant reviews? Every review on Faven is from someone who was actually there. We can prove it. Search for what you're actually looking for — and trust what you find."

Why Faven is Hard to Copy

  • Verification pipeline is the moat. Journey check + AI detection + EXIF reading + UTR validation takes time, trust, and data to build. First-mover advantage compounds here.
  • Creator credibility lock-in. A creator's verified score is built on real visits over time. They won't abandon it to start over on a competitor. Trust is sticky in a way that features aren't.
  • UPI as geographic moat. The UTR verification mechanism is uniquely powerful in India. A Western competitor cannot replicate this without deeply understanding the UPI ecosystem.
  • Network effects. More verified creators → better content → more users → more brand deals → more creator earnings → more creators. The flywheel accelerates with scale.
  • Data moat. The verified review database is the asset — not just the product. Every verified review posted makes every other feature more valuable. Search gets smarter. Brand matching gets better. Aesthetic AI gets more accurate. Everything compounds indefinitely.

Section 15

Local Dev Sequence

Follow this exact order to get the local dummy version running end-to-end.

Step 1 — Database

Install MySQL locally if not already installed. Create database faven_db. Update the .env file in the backend folder with your MySQL credentials.

Step 2 — Backend

Navigate to the backend folder. Run npm install. Run npm run seed to insert dummy restaurants and users. Run npm run dev. Confirm server is listening on port 3000 and seed data is visible in the logs.

Step 3 — Android Emulator

Open Android Studio. Start an Android Virtual Device (AVD) — a Pixel device running Android 13 or 14 works well. Wait for the emulator to fully boot.

Step 4 — Frontend

Navigate to the FavenApp folder. Run npm install. Run npx react-native run-android. The app builds and installs on the running emulator automatically.

Step 5 — Test the Full Flow

Register a new user on the login screen. Search for one of the 5 seeded restaurants. Tap through to Restaurant Detail. Submit a review with a photo and a UPI screenshot. Observe the verification badge assigned. Check the Profile screen for updated credibility score. Check the Leaderboard. The first post reward modal should appear on first verified submission.
